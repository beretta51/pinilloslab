Privacy Policy — App Shelf

Last updated: July 28, 2026

What this app does

App Shelf displays a row of app, game or project icons inside a Reddit post. The entries are created and curated by the moderators of the subreddit where the app is installed. Tapping an icon opens the link that a moderator set for it.

What data it stores

The app stores, within Reddit's own infrastructure (Devvit Redis), only the content a moderator typed or uploaded:

  • For each entry: its name, category, description, link, badge, and the address of its icon.
  • The heading shown above the row, and the order the entries appear in.

Icons uploaded by a moderator are stored on Reddit's own media hosting, not on any server operated by PinillosLab.

What it does NOT do

The app stores nothing whatsoever about the people who view the shelf. It does not record who opens the post, who taps an icon, or how many times. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it never sees your email, password, IP address, or any data beyond what Reddit exposes to apps.

The app also declares no permission to contact external servers, so it cannot send data anywhere outside Reddit's own platform even in principle. Its only permissions are storage, image upload, and the Reddit API at moderator scope.

Links to other sites

The icons on the shelf link to destinations chosen by the moderators — an app store page or a website, for example. Following one takes you off Reddit, and whatever you do on the destination site is governed by that site's own privacy policy, not by this one. The app is not told whether you followed a link.

Data deletion

Moderators can remove any entry from the shelf at any time, which permanently deletes it. Uninstalling the app from a subreddit deletes everything it stored for that subreddit. Because the app holds no personal data about viewers, there is nothing about you for it to delete.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. All processing happens on Reddit's Developer Platform and is additionally subject to Reddit's Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the moderators of the subreddit where the app is installed.
