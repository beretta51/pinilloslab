Overview

Dimmly is built to be private by default. No account. No tracking. No data sent to servers we control.

What Dimmly stores

All your lists, items, alarms, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework. We — the developer — never see this data.

iCloud sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Dimmly syncs your data across your devices using Apple’s CloudKit private database. This means:

  • Your data is stored in your own iCloud account, not on our servers.
  • We have no access to your synced data.
  • Sync is governed by Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy practices.
  • You can stop sync at any time from iOS Settings › Apple ID › iCloud › Dimmly.

Deleting the app

If you delete the app, your data is removed from your device. Data already in iCloud remains in your iCloud account unless you delete it from another device or from iCloud storage settings.

Notifications

Dimmly schedules local notifications for the alarms you set on items. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device through Apple’s notification system. No notification content is sent to any server.

Spotlight search

Dimmly indexes your lists and items into iOS Spotlight so you can find them from system search. This index lives on your device only.

CSV import and export

When you export a list, the resulting CSV file is shared through iOS’s standard share sheet — you decide where it goes. When you import a CSV, the file is read directly from the source you pick. We don’t see either file.

Purchases

Dimmly Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive transaction confirmation from Apple, but no payment information.

Analytics and tracking

Dimmly contains no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no tracking. The app does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Children

Dimmly is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
