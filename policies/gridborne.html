Information collection and use

Gridborne does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game is played entirely offline against an AI opponent. No account creation or login is required to play.

Game Center

Gridborne integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is managed entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your Game Center data.

Local data

Game preferences and statistics are stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since our app does not collect any data, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
