Overview

Percha is designed with privacy in mind. The app works entirely on your device — no account is required and no personal data is sent to external servers. Your wardrobe data syncs automatically across your Apple devices via iCloud, encrypted end-to-end.

Data we collect

Percha does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All information you enter (clothing items, photos, outfits, preferences) is stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework.

iCloud synchronization

Your wardrobe data (clothing items, outfits, usage history, preferences) is automatically synchronized to iCloud using Apple’s CloudKit service if you’re signed into your Apple account. This synchronization:

  • Is encrypted end-to-end by Apple
  • Happens transparently — no additional login required
  • Allows seamless access across your personal Apple devices
  • Is governed by Apple’s iCloud Privacy Policy

Permissions

Percha requests only the permissions it needs to work:

  • Camera — used only to photograph your clothing items. Photos are processed locally on your device (background removal, color detection) and saved to local storage. Photos are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Photo Library — used only to import existing photos of your clothing. Access is granted only when you choose to import a photo.
  • Location (When In Use) — used only to fetch local weather data to power outfit suggestions. Your location is sent directly to Apple’s WeatherKit service and is never stored or shared by Percha.
  • iCloud — syncs your wardrobe data across your Apple devices if you’re signed into iCloud. All data is encrypted by Apple.

Third-party services

Percha uses:

  • Apple WeatherKit — for weather-based outfit suggestions (governed by Apple’s privacy policy)
  • Apple CloudKit — for iCloud synchronization (governed by Apple’s iCloud Privacy Policy)

No other services

No other third-party services are used. Your data never leaves Apple’s ecosystem.

Data deletion

You can delete your Percha data at any time:

  • Local deletion — all data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • iCloud deletion — data synced to iCloud can be deleted by signing out of iCloud or removing Percha from iCloud settings in System Preferences. Apple retains backups according to their standard data retention policies.
