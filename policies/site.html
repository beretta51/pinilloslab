Overview

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or any kind of tracking. There is nothing to accept and nothing to opt out of.

What stays on your device

The site keeps one small piece of functional data in your browser’s session storage: a flag so the intro animation plays only once per session. It is not transmitted anywhere, it does not identify you, and it disappears when you close the browser.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address and message are delivered to me via Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which acts as a form-processing service. I use that information solely to reply to you. It is never sold, shared, or added to any mailing list.

Hosting
