No data collection

Solid does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — workout plans, exercise history, and session data — is stored on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account. No data ever passes through Solid’s servers, because Solid has no servers.

No accounts

Solid does not require you to create an account. There is no sign-in, no registration, and no user profile. iCloud sync uses your existing Apple ID exclusively — Solid never sees your credentials or identity.

No third-party services

Solid does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services. No data is ever sent to external servers.

On-device processing

Any intelligent features in the app (such as exercise parsing) run entirely on your device using Apple’s on-device frameworks. No text or input is sent to external servers.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Solid never has access to your iCloud data — syncing happens directly between your devices through Apple’s infrastructure.

Data deletion

To delete your data, disable iCloud sync in Settings and uninstall the app. If iCloud sync was enabled, you can also delete the app’s data from iCloud Drive in your iPhone Settings › Apple ID › iCloud › Manage Storage. Solid does not retain any information after deletion.
