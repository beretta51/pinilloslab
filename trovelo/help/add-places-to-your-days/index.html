Add places to your days

Every place you add becomes a card on one day of your trip, with a photo and how long it usually takes.

2 min read Updated Nov 1, 2026 iPhone iPad Mac
1

Open a day, then tap Add

Open your trip and tap a day in the strip at the top. Then tap + Add at the bottom of the screen.

1Pick a day2Tap + Add
2

Search for the place

Type its name or address. Results come from Apple Maps, near your trip’s city. Not there? Tap Search farther away.

1Near your trip2Your place3Look farther away
3

Tap +, and it’s in

Tap + next to the place. It turns into a green check, and the row tells you which day it’s on.

1Added2The day it’s on
4

Find it on your Board

The card lands on your day with a photo and how long it usually takes. The bar on top shows how full the day is.

1How full the day is2Your new card
5

Change it any time

Tap a card to open it and add a note, a file or a photo. Touch and hold it to move it: Pick a Date… sends it to another day, and Move to Unplanned keeps it for later.

1Notes and files2Another day3For later
1

Click Add card

In the Board, click + Add card at the bottom of a day’s column.

1
2

Search and pick the place

Type its name or address, then click + next to the place. It goes straight into that day. Rather use the keyboard?

↑↓Move ↩Add ⌘↩Add and close ⌘ZUndo escClose
3

Drag cards between days

Drag any card to another column to change its day, or up and down to change the order.

1

Good to know

An empty day offers handpicked spots for your city, ready to add.

Got a list from a blog or ChatGPT? Tap Paste your list on an empty day.

Not sure which day yet? Keep it in Unplanned, the tray next to the days.
