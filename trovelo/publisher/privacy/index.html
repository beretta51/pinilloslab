Trovelo Publisher · Mac

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

Trovelo Publisher is a Mac app made by Pinillos Lab (Eduardo Pinillos, independent developer) that creates travel carousels and short videos for Trovelo. Pinillos Lab runs no servers for it: your work stays on your Mac (and in your own iCloud Drive, if you use it), and the developer never receives, stores, sells or shares your data. When you connect TikTok or Pinterest, the only things that leave your Mac are the videos and images you choose to send, and they go straight to TikTok or Pinterest.

1. Who is responsible

Eduardo Pinillos (Pinillos Lab), Portugal · nocion.exento_8u@icloud.com

2. When you connect TikTok

  • Sign-in: you sign in on TikTok's own page. Trovelo Publisher never sees your password.
  • What we receive from TikTok: your basic profile (display name, profile picture and an anonymous account ID), only to show you which account is connected, plus the access tokens that let the app send drafts on your behalf.
  • Where it is kept: only on your Mac, in a private file that only your user account can read (never in iCloud or on any server).
  • What we send to TikTok: only the video files you choose to send, uploaded directly from your Mac to TikTok's servers as drafts in your TikTok inbox. Nothing is posted until you post it yourself in the TikTok app.
  • What we never access: your existing videos, followers, messages, likes or analytics.
  • Disconnecting: disconnect in Trovelo Publisher (this deletes the tokens from your Mac) or remove the app's access in TikTok's settings, under the apps connected to your account.

What TikTok does with your content is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. When you connect Pinterest

  • Sign-in: you sign in and approve access on Pinterest's own page. Trovelo Publisher never sees your password.
  • Permissions it asks for: read your account name, read and create boards, and create Pins. Pinterest requires its "read Pins" permission to create a Pin; the app never reads your existing Pins.
  • What we receive from Pinterest: your username, only to show you which account is connected; the names of your boards, so you can choose where each Pin goes; the ID of each Pin the app creates, so you can open it; and the access tokens that let the app create Pins on your behalf.
  • Where it is kept: only on your Mac, in a private file that only your user account can read (never in iCloud or on any server).
  • What we send to Pinterest: only what you choose, one Pin at a time: when you pick a carousel and send it, its images, title, description, alt text and link go directly from your Mac to Pinterest as a Pin on the board you chose. If you ask the app to create a board, it sends that board's name. Nothing is posted automatically or in bulk.
  • What we never access: your existing Pins, followers, messages, ads or analytics.
  • Disconnecting: disconnect in Trovelo Publisher (this deletes the tokens from your Mac) or remove the app's access in Pinterest's settings, under the apps connected to your account. Pins you created stay on Pinterest until you delete them there.

What Pinterest does with your content is covered by the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

4. Other services the app uses

  • Pexels: to find photos and videos, the app sends search words (place names) to Pexels and downloads the results. Pexels privacy policy.
  • Music: songs are downloaded from incompetech.com; nothing about you is sent.
  • Google Drive: if you choose a Google Drive folder, the app copies finished files into that folder on your Mac, and Google Drive for desktop uploads them to your own Google account.
  • Claude: if you use the writing features, the text and images involved are processed by Claude, by Anthropic, through your own Claude account, under Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Voice-over: spoken narration is generated on your Mac; nothing is sent.

5. What we don't do

  • No accounts with Pinillos Lab, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, and no use of it to train AI models.

6. How long data is kept

Your projects stay on your Mac (or your iCloud Drive) until you delete them. TikTok and Pinterest tokens stay on your Mac until you disconnect or they expire.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can access, correct and delete your data. Because everything is on your own devices, you can do this directly: delete projects in the app, and disconnect TikTok or Pinterest to delete their tokens. For any question, write to the email above.

8. Children

Trovelo Publisher is not meant for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age to use TikTok or Pinterest in their country.
