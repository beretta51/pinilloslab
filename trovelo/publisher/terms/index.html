1. What Trovelo Publisher is

Trovelo Publisher is a Mac app made by Pinillos Lab (Eduardo Pinillos, independent developer). It creates travel carousels and short travel videos for Trovelo, a travel-planning app, and can send them to social networks you connect.

On TikTok, Trovelo Publisher only sends videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted until you open the draft in the TikTok app, review it, edit it if you want, and post it yourself.
